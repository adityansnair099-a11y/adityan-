# Stakeholder Roadmap for the SkyCity Restaurant Portfolio Project

## 1. Project Objective
To analyze restaurant performance across the SkyCity portfolio, identify profit drivers and cost pressures, and provide actionable recommendations that support better business decisions.

## 2. Strategic Roadmap

| Phase | Focus Area | Key Activities | Stakeholder Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Business Understanding | Define the problem, scope, and business questions | Clear project purpose and expected value |
| 2 | Data Collection & Validation | Review sales, profit/loss, cost, and operational data | Reliable dataset for decision-making |
| 3 | Performance Analysis | Assess revenue, margin, cost drivers, and outlet performance | Insights into what is driving results |
| 4 | Insight Development | Compare restaurants, identify trends, and diagnose underperformance | Evidence-based business findings |
| 5 | Recommendations | Prioritize actions such as cost control, pricing, staffing, and menu optimization | Concrete improvement plan |
| 6 | Reporting & Presentation | Build dashboards and board-ready summary | Executive visibility and alignment |
| 7 | Monitoring | Track KPIs and business impact after recommendations | Continuous improvement and accountability |

## 3. Suggested Timeline

| Timeline | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Project kickoff, scope alignment, confirm KPIs |
| Week 2 | Data audit, cleaning, and validation |
| Week 3 | Revenue and profitability analysis |
| Week 4 | Cost driver and variance analysis |
| Week 5 | Outlet and category benchmarking |
| Week 6 | Insight generation and recommendation drafting |
| Week 7 | Dashboard and presentation development |
| Week 8 | Stakeholder presentation and final recommendations |

## 4. Key Questions the Project Will Answer
- Which restaurants are most profitable?
- Which outlets are underperforming and why?
- Which costs are increasing fastest?
- Which products or categories drive the best margins?
- Which time periods perform best or worst?
- Where should management focus improvement efforts?

## 5. KPIs to Track
- Total revenue
- Gross profit
- Net profit
- Profit margin by outlet
- Labor cost percentage
- Food and beverage cost percentage
- Revenue per restaurant
- Sales trend by month or period
- Category contribution to revenue and profit

## 6. Expected Deliverables
- Clean and validated data set
- Performance analysis summary
- Profitability and cost analysis
- Benchmark report by outlet
- Dashboard or management report
- Executive presentation
- Recommendations with priority ranking

## 7. Stakeholder Presentation Structure
Use this simple flow in your meeting:

1. Executive Summary
2. Business Context
3. Key Findings
4. Performance Drivers
5. Risks and Opportunities
6. Recommended Actions
7. Next Steps and Timeline

## 8. Suggested Message for Stakeholders
“This project will provide a clear understanding of the restaurant portfolio’s financial performance, identify operational opportunities, and support data-driven decisions that improve profitability and efficiency.”

## 9. Final Recommendation
For stakeholders, the strongest presentation is:
- simple,
- insight-led,
- action-oriented,
- and focused on business impact.
