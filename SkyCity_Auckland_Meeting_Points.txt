SKYCITY AUCKLAND RESTAURANT PORTFOLIO
Meeting Points

KEY FINDINGS

- The dataset records $77.74 million in revenue and $7.87 million in net profit, an aggregate margin of 10.1%. The currency and reporting period are not specified.
- Full-service requires closer review: 408 of 452 records in this format are loss-making, with an aggregate margin of -5.8%. These are all the loss-making records in the dataset.
- Uber Eats and DoorDash account for 61.2% of recorded revenue but have a combined calculated net margin of 0.86%. In-store and self-delivery margins are 26.8% and 22.9%, respectively.
- Regional margins are relatively close, from 9.5% in the CBD to 10.5% in West Auckland. Differences by format are more pronounced in this dataset.

RECOMMENDED DISCUSSION

1. Validate the underlying channel-profit and cost-allocation calculations.
2. Review Full-service performance at outlet level before making broad portfolio decisions.
3. Examine platform fees, delivery costs, and channel contribution before changing channel strategy.
4. Add verified currency, reporting period, and methodology, and collect employment and business-survival data before using these results for public-policy or funding decisions.

MEETING TAKEAWAY

The portfolio is profitable overall, but that headline masks concentrated Full-service losses and thin platform-channel margins. Validate the calculations first, then target a deeper review where the data points.

CAVEAT

This is a single dataset with no stated reporting period, currency, or independently audited methodology. It does not prove that delivery platforms cause lower profitability or represent the wider hospitality sector.
