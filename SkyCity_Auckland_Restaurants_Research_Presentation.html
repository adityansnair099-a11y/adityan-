SkyCity Portfolio Review

1. Research Objective

  • Evaluate how operating model, restaurant format, and customer location influence sales and profitability.
  • Examine the relationship between digital channel growth, commissions, and delivery cost pressure.
  • Identify clear actions for better portfolio management and more resilient unit economics.
Objective

2. Dataset and Business Context

Data included

  • Cuisine type, format, subregion, and growth factor
  • Monthly orders and average order value
  • Revenue by channel
  • COGS, OPEX, commission rate, and delivery cost

Why it matters

  • Top-line revenue is not enough without margin analysis
  • Delivery increases reach but adds cost and pressure
  • Portfolio strategy depends on unit economics
Context

3. Exploratory Findings

  • Digital growth is a double-edged sword: stronger demand but higher commission and delivery costs.
  • Format matters: QSR and café models often support repeat ordering and efficient volume.
  • Location shapes demand: subregions vary in customer behavior and cost-to-serve.
  • The strongest units: manage AOV, cost discipline, and channel mix together.
EDA

4. Key Insights

Main performance patterns

  • Delivery-heavy stores can lose profit even while growing revenue.
  • High volume does not always mean high profitability.
  • Strong in-store economics are the most resilient base.

Strategic implication

  • Portfolio decisions should be guided by contribution margin.
  • Low-margin growth must be challenged with pricing and menu redesign.
  • Best-performing stores balance accessibility and cost discipline.
Insights

5. Recommendations

  • Protect and expand high-margin in-store channels before over-investing in delivery-heavy growth.
  • Use delivery strategically where it supports acquisition and convenience without margin erosion.
  • Review underperforming restaurants by format, subregion, and commission burden before scaling investment.
  • Improve menu design and pricing architecture to sustain AOV while controlling cost-to-serve.
Recommendations

6. Conclusion

  • Portfolio performance is strongest when growth is aligned with unit-level profitability discipline.
  • The key question is not only how much a store sells, but how much it retains after commissions, delivery costs, and operating expenses.
  • A data-led portfolio strategy can improve profitability by reallocating investment to formats and locations with stronger resilience.
Conclusion
